Returns & Refunds

Last updated October 28, 2026

Made-to-order items

Because each item is printed just for you, we cannot accept returns or exchanges for change of mind, or for the wrong size or colour ordered.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, misprinted or incorrect, email sj@monarchofthemoon.shop within 30 days of delivery with your order number and a clear photo of the problem. We will send a free replacement or refund you, whichever you prefer.

Lost orders

If tracking shows your order is lost or has not arrived 30 days after it shipped, contact us and we will reprint it or refund you.

Cancellations and changes

Orders go into production quickly. If you need to cancel or change an order, email us as soon as possible. We can only help if production has not begun.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Stripe. Banks usually take 5–10 business days to show the refund.
