Privacy Policy

Last updated October 28, 2026

Monarch of the Moon (“we”, Ontario, Canada) respects your privacy. Questions: sj@monarchofthemoon.shop.

What we collect

  • Order details you give at checkout: name, email, phone number, shipping address and the items you buy.
  • Payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card number.
  • Your shopping cart, saved in your own browser (local storage) so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent to us until you check out.

How we use it

Only to take payment, make and ship your order, send order and shipping updates, and answer your questions. We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing email.

Who receives it

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • Printful and Printify receive your name, address, email and phone number so they can print and deliver your order, and share it with the carriers.
  • Cloudflare runs our order server and GitHub Pages hosts this website; both handle ordinary web request data such as your IP address.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts on this site, so your browser contacts Google when pages load.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as needed to fulfil orders, handle returns and meet tax and accounting rules.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing sj@monarchofthemoon.shop. Some records may need to be kept by law.

Children

This shop is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
